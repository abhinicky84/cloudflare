Current gap diagnosis

What is holding the experience back?

The site appears to have a solid enterprise foundation, but several customer-facing and operational gaps limit its ability to serve diverse users, guide high-intent traffic, and create measurable digital outcomes.

Content management maturity

  • Limited reuse of modular content
  • Opportunity for stronger governance
  • Need scalable localization workflows

Limited personalization

  • Mostly static user experience
  • Limited audience-based messaging
  • Underused Adobe Target potential

Shallow navigation

  • L1-heavy top navigation
  • Limited deep links to key journeys
  • More clicks than needed

Search underperformance

  • Basic results experience
  • Limited filtering and suggestions
  • Missed support-deflection opportunity

Responsive and visual QA

  • Hero image cropping issues
  • Mobile layout QA opportunities
  • Need stronger visual standards

Analytics and customer feedback

  • Limited visible feedback loops
  • Need stronger funnel dashboards
  • Search and form insights can improve

Future initiatives for Lumen

Each recommendation is written for business stakeholders first, with a technical overview that can help development, architecture, analytics, and operations teams understand the implementation path.

Recommendation 01

Launch Spanish localization

Make CenturyLink.com inclusive and discoverable for Spanish-speaking customers in the US market.

Business value

  • Reach a larger US audience
  • Improve trust and accessibility
  • Grow Spanish SEO traffic

Technical approach

  • Create AEM language copies
  • Add language switcher and hreflang
  • Define translation governance

Recommended initiative sequence

The sequencing below balances business value, implementation effort, stakeholder visibility, and whether the work can be delivered within the current AEM-centered stream.

Initiative Business value Technical direction Timing Priority
Responsive image and visual QA Quick brand-quality improvement. Breakpoint, image, and template QA. Short-term Very high
Spanish localization Reach Spanish-speaking customers. AEM language copies and hreflang. Short to mid-term Very high
